## Shared generation brief

- Use case: photorealistic-natural website photography, premium but believable Macau travel setting.
- Vehicle body references: user-supplied `images (1).jpeg`, `images (2).jpeg`, `images (3).jpeg`, and `40alphard_b_sp_09.jpg`. Preserve the Alphard 40 Series tall, upright body, broad layered grille, narrow headlights, sculpted front bumper, and sliding-door profile.
- Wheel exception: use ordinary factory-style Toyota Alphard alloy wheels, normal tire size, and normal ride height. Do not copy the modified wheels or lowered stance in the references.
- Exclude WALD branding and plate, aftermarket badging, readable license plates, watermarks, invented business signs, and identifiable partner logos.
- Heroes: wide 16:9, quiet dark upper area for white copy and booking/inquiry form; show the intended audience experiencing the service rather than a vehicle-only catalog shot.
- Local Chauffeur hero exception: an interior rear-passenger viewpoint looking toward a fictional right-hand-drive chauffeur on a Macau road; exterior body and wheel details are not visible in that frame.
- Featured images: wide editorial crop for the main narrative section. Show a different moment from the page's hero. Do not imply confirmed cross-border eligibility, attraction guiding, wedding decoration, or actual B2B partner relationships.
