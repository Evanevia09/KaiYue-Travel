## Company Overview

**Company name:** KAI YUE TRAVEL GROUP  
**Chinese name:** 凱悅旅遊集團  
**Portuguese name:** GRUPO DE VIAGENS KAI YUE  
**Location:** Macau SAR  
**Established:** 2009  
**Tourism license:** Macau Tourism Bureau license No. 0162  
**Source-reported license validity:** 2026-09-20

Kai Yue Travel Group presents itself as a Macau-based premium travel and chauffeur transportation company focused on luxury private-car and charter services. Its stated service philosophy emphasizes safety, comfort, punctuality, professionalism, and premium guest service.

The source website positions Kai Yue as serving individual travelers, VIP guests, corporate clients, hotels, events, and tourism-related partners in Macau and the Greater Bay Area.

## Core Services

### Luxury Chauffeur / Private Charter

Kai Yue's primary offering is premium chauffeured transportation in Macau, with the Toyota Alphard 40 Series positioned as its flagship vehicle.

### Airport Transfers

Professional airport pickup and drop-off services with scheduled chauffeur collection and transfer to the guest's destination.

### Hotel Transfers

The website's service terms describe hotel pickup and drop-off as part of the company's transportation offering.

### Corporate Transportation

Premium vehicle and chauffeur services for business travel, executives, corporate guests, and company transportation requirements.

The company also promotes customized, compliant, one-stop corporate mobility solutions.

### Macau Sightseeing

Private sightseeing transportation around Macau, positioned for guests who want to explore the city with a dedicated vehicle and chauffeur.

### Customized Chauffeur Services

Customized private-car arrangements based on itinerary and client requirements.

### Wedding Transportation

Dedicated vehicle services for weddings and ceremonial occasions.

### VIP Transportation

The company positions itself for VIP reception and high-end guest transportation, including performers, executives, hotel guests, and event VIPs.

### Large Event Fleet Operations

Kai Yue presents fleet coordination, chauffeur management, and VIP reception for large events as a core operational capability.

## Flagship Vehicle

### Toyota Alphard 40 Series Executive Lounge

The Toyota Alphard 40 Series is the main vehicle promoted by the company.

**Source-listed specifications:**

- 7 seats
- Vehicle length: 5,010 mm
- Wheelbase: 3,000 mm
- Executive Lounge configuration
- Massage seats

The website positions this vehicle for luxury transfers, VIP transportation, corporate travel, tourism, and event use.

## Fleet Capability

A published case study states that Kai Yue deployed **more than 15 Toyota Alphard 40 Series vehicles** for the 2025 Macau International Comedy Festival VIP transportation project.

This does not necessarily represent the company's total owned fleet size. It should be treated as evidence of demonstrated deployment capacity rather than a confirmed current fleet count.
